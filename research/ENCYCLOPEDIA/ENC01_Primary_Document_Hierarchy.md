# Encyclopedia 01 — Primary Document Hierarchy
## Definition
A primary document is an original record created by a party with direct knowledge or authority: statute, regulation, notice of award, debarment letter, FOIA email corpus, IC declassified assessment, hearing transcript, peer-reviewed paper, court filing.
## Why it matters
Without hierarchy, media summaries become "evidence" and SOLID collapses into narrative control.
## Hierarchy (strongest → weakest for Meridian)
1. Court orders / debarment notices / NoA / regulation text
2. FOIA-produced emails and attachments
3. IC declassified assessments (with confidence labels)
4. Congressional hearing transcripts and exhibits
5. Peer-reviewed scientific literature
6. Agency guidance pages (versioned)
7. Secondary journalism (never SOLID alone)
## Operational rule
M4: SOLID only with (1)–(5). Secondary may inform CONTESTED framing only.
## Pitfalls
- Treating SSCP staff inference as primary science
- Treating CIA lean without "low confidence" as high certainty
## KPI
Every SOLID claim lists at least one primary URL or archive hash.
