# ENC36 — User Sovereignty Over Local Environment (Lawful Core)

## Theory
Data delivered to user hardware may be filtered/re-rendered by the owner for privacy and usability (mute the ad, block the tracker script locally).

## Boundary
Sovereignty over **local interpretation** ≠ license to:
- defeat authentication/paywalls
- pool credentials
- spoof bot-defense fingerprints to access non-public resources
- deceive integrity checks to run unauthorized hooks on third-party security surfaces

## Charter / Bill of Rights note
Constitutional/Charter rights primarily constrain **government**. Private platforms' ToS and access-control laws still apply. Use as rights-protector framing for **state** overreach and **user privacy against forced telemetry**, not as a skeleton key.

# ENC37 — Interoperability / Anti-Circumvention Nuance

DMCA 1201 and analogs restrict circumvention of technological protection measures, with limited exceptions (e.g. some interoperability, security research, accessibility — jurisdiction-dependent).

## Rule for Meridian
Do not treat carve-outs as blanket authorization for production bypass of paywalls, DRM, or edge bot gates. Fact-specific legal advice is for a lawyer; swarm documents **scope**, not exploit recipes.
