## Detailed Claim-by-Claim

### C01: COVID-19 most likely emerged from a laboratory/research-related accident at WIV

- **Report section**: origins_lab_leak / proximal_origin
- **Report evidence**: 5 circumstantial arguments listed (FCS 'not found in nature', single introduction, WIV GoF + biosafety, WIV illnesses Fall 2019, absence of natural intermediate). Proximal Origin prompted by Fauci.
- **Cross-check**: IC divided (CIA 2025 low-conf lab-leak; FBI/DOE earlier lab; others natural/undecided). SAGO 2025 leans zoonotic. Peer-reviewed (Worobey/Pekar) strong market clustering. No smoking-gun sample or notebook. EcoHealth viruses genetically distant. FCS has natural analogs (BANAL). SOLID as hypothesis; NOT SOLID as settled fact.
- **Primary docs cited**: Emails Fauci/Andersen Feb 2020; EcoHealth Year 5 report; DEFUSE proposal; State cables; intelligence assessments
